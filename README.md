# MiniSurrender

Simple addon that makes /afk and /gg surrender arena.

This is much faster than having to right click the pvp icon and choose surrender or leave and then click the confirm popup.

That's really all there is to it.

[Discord](https://discord.gg/UruPTPHHxK)

## Download

Available on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/minisurrender).
